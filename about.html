About Hamilton

Hamilton: An American Musical is a groundbreaking musical written by Lin-Manuel Miranda. It tells the story of American Founding Father Alexander Hamilton through a blend of hip-hop, R&B, pop, and traditional Broadway styles.

Premiered Off-Broadway in February 2015 and moved to Broadway in August 2015. It won 11 Tony Awards, a Pulitzer Prize, and a Grammy Award.

The show revolutionized musical theater by featuring a diverse cast to portray the Founding Fathers and Mothers of America.
