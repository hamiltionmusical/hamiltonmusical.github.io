About Hamilton

Hamilton: An American Musical is a sung-and-rapped-through musical that tells the story of American Founding Father Alexander Hamilton using a diverse cast and a blend of hip-hop, R&B, pop, and traditional show tunes.

It premiered Off-Broadway in 2015 and moved to Broadway later that year, winning 11 Tony Awards including Best Musical.

The Story

From his humble beginnings in the Caribbean to his rise as George Washington's right-hand man and first Secretary of the Treasury, Hamilton's story is told with energy, passion, and revolutionary spirit.
